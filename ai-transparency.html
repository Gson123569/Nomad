Verification Promise

Every factual claim about tax rates, residency requirements, or legal obligations is verified against at least two independent primary sources before publication. We do not rely on AI for factual accuracy.

Explainability Statement

How We Use AI

This site employs Large Language Models for structural drafting and content organization. However, all tax figures, legal interpretations, and strategic recommendations are researched and verified by human subject matter experts.

Specifically, AI assists with:

  • Generating initial content outlines and structure
  • Suggesting clearer ways to explain complex concepts
  • Proofreading for grammar and readability
  • Writing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript code
  • Summarizing lengthy source documents for human review

AI never: Makes final content decisions, provides personalized advice, or interprets tax law without human verification.

Bias Mitigation

We actively test for and mitigate two key types of bias:

Geographic Bias

We ensure our content does not privilege Western jurisdictions. Our coverage includes emerging markets, Asian financial centers, and developing nations on equal footing.

Wealth Bias

We verify that strategies work for emerging wealth and mid-net-worth individuals, not just established ultra-high-net-worth families.

Ethics Framework

"We adhere to the principle that refusing to deploy AI unethically is as important as deploying it ethically."

— Nomad Wealth Atlas AI Governance Charter

We Do NOT Use AI For:

  • ✕
    Personalized tax advice

    Requires a licensed accountant who knows your specific situation

  • ✕
    Legal interpretation

    Requires bar admission and professional liability insurance

  • ✕
    Investment recommendations

    Requires fiduciary status and regulatory compliance

  • ✕
    Predicting individual user behavior

    We do not profile users or make predictions about individuals

We DO Use AI For:

  • ✓
    Content structuring and organization

    Helping present information in clear, logical formats

  • ✓
    Language refinement and clarity

    Improving readability while preserving technical accuracy

  • ✓
    Research assistance

    Summarizing sources, always human-verified before use

  • ✓
    Code generation for calculators

    Technical implementation, human-reviewed for accuracy
