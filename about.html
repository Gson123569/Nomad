About Nomad Wealth Atlas

Independent, verified information for global citizens navigating tax and residency decisions.

Our Mission

Nomad Wealth Atlas exists to cut through the noise of conflicting tax advice and marketing claims. We provide rigorously verified, jurisdiction-neutral information for location-independent professionals, entrepreneurs, and investors making high-stakes decisions about where to structure their lives and assets. Unlike commercial services that steer clients toward specific jurisdictions, we present facts—verified, cross-referenced, and regularly updated—so you can make decisions based on reality, not sales pitches.

How We Verify Information

Every data point on this site follows a rigorous verification protocol. We don't rely on secondhand reports or promotional materials.

📚 Primary Sources

OECD tax databases, official government gazettes, bilateral treaty texts, and regulatory filings. We go to the source, not the summary.

✓ Cross-Reference Rule

Every significant claim is verified against a minimum of three independent sources before publication.

🔄 Update Cadence

Tax rates: Quarterly review. Regulatory changes: Monthly monitoring. Breaking developments: As they occur.

👤 Expert Review

All content is reviewed by qualified tax professionals and legal experts before publication.
